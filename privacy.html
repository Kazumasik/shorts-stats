Privacy Policy

Shorts Stats · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who uses this tool

Shorts Stats is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who connects his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's OAuth consent screen, the tool accesses:

  • analytics of the owner's own YouTube channels and videos (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers gained, traffic sources, audience geography);
  • metadata of the owner's own videos (titles, descriptions, publish dates, privacy status);
  • the ability to upload videos to the owner's own channels.

It does not access data of any other YouTube user.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to compare the owner's own videos and to publish them. It is stored on the owner's own computer and in his private work environment. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Google API Services

Shorts Stats uses YouTube API Services. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Stored data is deleted on request, or within 30 days after access is revoked.
